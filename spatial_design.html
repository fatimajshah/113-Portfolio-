Sundial Design

A projecting sundial that filters sunlight through calibrated apertures and casts the hour onto a central platform.

It functions accurately on three days:

  • Summer Solstice
  • October 2nd (Project Critique Day)
  • Winter Solstice

Designated for South Hadley, MA

Process:

  1. Studied the Sun’s Altitude and Azimuth on the chosen days.
  2. Placed apertures according to those angles.
  3. Each aperture admits light at its corresponding hour.
  4. The projected beam lands on the central platform.
